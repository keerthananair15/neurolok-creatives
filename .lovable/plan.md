# Neurolok Premium Light Theme

## Goal
Convert the existing Neurolok creative workspace to a premium Apple-inspired light theme without changing its navigation, creation workflow, page structure, or behavior.

## Visual system
- Replace deep-black surfaces with white and warm off-white layers, deep charcoal text, quiet gray borders, and restrained emerald accents.
- Use Helvetica as the primary interface font, with a distinctive display treatment reserved for the Neurolok brand and selected headlines. If Nofex is not available as an approved local/web font, use a close premium display fallback rather than introducing an unlicensed file.
- Rework glass surfaces into translucent white panels with subtle blur, thin highlights, soft neutral shadows, and faint emerald reflections.
- Remove neon-style glow and heavy dark gradients while preserving tasteful depth and motion.

## Workspace updates
- Restyle the desktop sidebar, mobile menu, active navigation, Credits, Ask Suggestion, and Profile while keeping their current positions and actions.
- Preserve the prompt composer as the primary interaction, using a clear white liquid-glass surface and restrained emerald selection states.
- Refresh the Home hero with a light abstract liquid-glass form behind the existing layered creative previews.
- Restyle generated image/video tiles and quick-create cards as editorial media cards with soft shadows and legible glass overlays.
- Apply the light token system consistently across Create, Storyboard, Characters, Campaigns, Trends, Library, Settings, asset detail, authentication, dialogs, inputs, and empty states.

## Motion and accessibility
- Retain smooth page and card transitions, tuning shadows and movement for the lighter theme.
- Preserve reduced-motion support, visible focus states, readable contrast, and responsive behavior.

## Validation
- Check the authenticated Home workspace and representative creation/library pages at desktop and mobile widths.
- Confirm the light surfaces, media overlays, sidebar, composer, typography, interactions, and route transitions render correctly without overlap or console errors.
